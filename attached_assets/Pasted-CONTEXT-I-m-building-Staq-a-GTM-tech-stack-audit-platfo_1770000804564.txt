CONTEXT:
I'm building Staq, a GTM tech stack audit platform. We have a conversational intake system powered by Claude API. The intake system already has tool intelligence modules for ZoomInfo, Apollo, Sales Navigator, Gong, and Salesforce in /server/tool-intelligence/.

HubSpot is the other CRM option (alongside Salesforce). The intake flow asks "Which CRM do you use?" early in the conversation. If they say HubSpot, we need Hub-aware and tier-aware follow-ups.

Per the STAQ_CONVERSATIONAL_INTAKE_FRAMEWORK.pdf:
- If CRM = HubSpot, ask HubSpot-specific questions about tiers, Hubs, integrations
- Skip HubSpot questions entirely if they use Salesforce
- Probe vague answers and flag uncertainty for screen share

Per the STAQ_MVP_SPECIFICATION.pdf:
- Keep conversation under 20 exchanges total
- Generate JSON summary + preliminary analysis when complete

GOAL:
Add HubSpot CRM intelligence to the intake system so that when a prospect mentions HubSpot as their CRM, the system demonstrates expertise through Hub-aware and tier-aware questions—without giving away diagnostic value.

REQUIREMENTS:

1. Create /server/tool-intelligence/hubspot.js following the same structure as existing modules

2. HubSpot has a unique architecture—Hubs + Tiers:
   
   Hubs (can buy independently or bundle):
   - Marketing Hub: Email, automation, landing pages, ads
   - Sales Hub: Sequences, quotes, forecasting, meeting scheduler
   - Service Hub: Ticketing, knowledge base, customer portal
   - Content Hub: CMS, blog, website
   - Data Hub: Data quality, sync, operations
   - Commerce Hub: Payments, invoicing, CPQ
   
   Tiers (apply to each Hub):
   - Free: Very limited
   - Starter: Basic paid ($20/mo/seat)
   - Professional: Real automation, sequences, custom reports ($90/mo/seat for Sales/Service)
   - Enterprise: Custom objects, advanced features ($150/mo/seat for Sales/Service)

3. Conversation rules when user says "HubSpot" for CRM:

   First mention response:
   "HubSpot—nice. Which Hubs do you have? Just Sales Hub, or are you running Marketing and Service too?"
   
   If they don't know which Hubs:
   "No worries—a lot of people inherit a HubSpot setup and aren't sure what's included. Do you know if you're on Starter, Professional, or Enterprise?"
   
   If they know Hubs but not tier:
   "Got it. Do you know if you're on Professional or Enterprise? That determines a lot of what you can actually do in the platform."
   
   If Starter tier:
   "Starter—so you're probably hitting some walls with automation and sequences. Is that a pain point, or is it working for what you need?"
   
   If Professional tier:
   "Professional—that's the sweet spot for most teams. Are you actually using the automation and sequences, or has that been hard to get off the ground?"
   
   If Enterprise tier:
   "Enterprise—full platform. Are you using custom objects, or mostly just the standard setup?"

4. Satisfaction question uses insider framing:
   "On a scale of 1-5, how's HubSpot working for your team? 1 being 'we've outgrown it' and 5 being 'love it, wouldn't switch.'"

5. Score responses:
   - 4-5: "Nice—sounds like it's a good fit." → Move on to tool inventory
   - 3: "A 3 with HubSpot usually means one of two things: you're hitting feature limits and need to upgrade, or you're paying for stuff you're not using. Which one feels closer?" → ONE follow-up max
   - 1-2: "Got it—what's the main frustration? Is it capability limits, or is it more about how it's set up?" → Probe, then flag for screen share

6. Named failure modes (safe to reference as patterns):
   - "A lot of teams end up on HubSpot Starter and get frustrated when they can't do sequences or custom reports—those require Professional"
   - "I see a lot of HubSpot setups where Marketing Hub is fully built out but Sales Hub is barely used"
   - "Marketing contact pricing catches people off guard—you grow your list and suddenly your bill doubles"
   - "The free CRM is great to start, but teams often outgrow it faster than they expect"
   - "HubSpot's easy to get started with, but the complexity sneaks up on you at scale"

7. NEVER say:
   - Specific pricing for any tier or Hub
   - Feature utilization benchmarks (e.g., "67% feature adoption is healthy")
   - How to fix tier limitations
   - Tier recommendations (e.g., "you should upgrade to Professional")
   - HubSpot vs. Salesforce comparison recommendations

8. Integration health probes (use when other tools are mentioned):
   When Gong mentioned + HubSpot CRM:
   "Is Gong syncing call data back to HubSpot deals, or is that connection still on the to-do list?"
   
   When Outreach/SalesLoft mentioned + HubSpot CRM:
   "Are you running sequences in Outreach, or do you use HubSpot's native sequences? Or both?"
   
   When ZoomInfo/Apollo mentioned + HubSpot CRM:
   "Is your enrichment data flowing into HubSpot contacts automatically?"
   
   When Salesforce also mentioned (rare but happens):
   "Wait—you have both HubSpot and Salesforce? Which one is the source of truth for your sales team?"

9. Overlap detection with HubSpot native features:
   If they have HubSpot Sales Hub Professional+ AND Outreach/SalesLoft:
   "You've got sequences in HubSpot Sales Hub. Is there a reason you're also running Outreach, or is that something that evolved over time?"
   
   If they have HubSpot Marketing Hub AND separate marketing automation:
   "Interesting—so you have Marketing Hub but also use {other tool} for marketing. How do those work together?"
   
   (Don't probe aggressively—just note for screen share if mentioned)

10. Skip logic integration:
    - If CRM = Salesforce, skip ALL HubSpot questions
    - If CRM = "Other", note as finding and skip CRM-specific probes

11. Screen share flags to auto-generate:
    - satisfaction <= 2 → "PRIORITY: HubSpot dissatisfaction—deep dive on {pain_point}"
    - tier = "Starter" AND pain_point contains "can't" or "limited" → "Likely hitting tier feature limits—review upgrade vs. alternatives"
    - tier = "don't know" OR hubs = "don't know" → "Verify HubSpot Hubs and tier—unclear what they're paying for"
    - pain_point contains "expensive" or "cost" or "pricing" → "Review contact pricing and seat utilization"
    - has Marketing Hub AND has Sales Hub AND mentioned "not using" one of them → "Hub underutilization—potential cost savings"
    - no dedicated admin → "Flag operational risk—no clear HubSpot owner"
    - has HubSpot Sales Hub Pro+ AND has Outreach/SalesLoft → "Potential sequence tool redundancy"
    - mentioned "outgrown" → "Evaluate if HubSpot is still the right fit or if migration should be discussed"

12. Update /server/tool-intelligence/index.js to include hubspot in the registry with triggers: ["hubspot", "hub spot", "hs crm"]
