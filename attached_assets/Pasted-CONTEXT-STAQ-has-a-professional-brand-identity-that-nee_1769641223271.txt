CONTEXT:
STAQ has a professional brand identity that needs to be applied across the platform. The current design is bland and doesn't reflect our brand. I'm providing the complete design system below.

GOAL:
Redesign the landing page and chat interface to match our brand identity. Make it feel premium, trustworthy, and visually distinctive.

## STAQ BRAND DESIGN SYSTEM

### Color Palette (use these exact values)
- Bright Cyan: #00D4E8 (highlights, hover states, accents)
- Primary Cyan: #00B4C4 (primary buttons, links, logo)
- Deep Cyan: #0099A8 (shadows, depth, secondary elements, button hover states)
- Midnight: #1A1A2E (dark backgrounds, headlines, contrast sections)
- White: #FFFFFF (backgrounds, cards)
- Light Gray: #F8F9FA (subtle backgrounds)
- Soft Cyan Tint: #F0FDFF (chat background)

### Typography
- Headlines: Bold (700), tight letter-spacing (-1px or -2px)
- Body: Regular (400), comfortable line-height (1.6)
- Use system-ui, sans-serif font stack

### Spacing & Radius
- Border radius: 8px for cards, 6px for buttons, 4px for small elements
- Consistent padding: 16px, 24px, 32px, 48px scale
